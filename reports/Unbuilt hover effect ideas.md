# Pick the hovers the lab skipped

The lab can still add **twenty-six** hover effects that are not among the forty-two cards already shipped. **Twelve** play on a logo and on a letter-by-letter headline, **ten** should be built for the headline only, and **four** should be built for the logo only. Each one can run in Canvas 2D or GSAP, disturb the artwork while the pointer is inside the card, and return to rest on leave. The shared radius does real work on the Canvas lenses: a rim-refraction droplet, a chromatic rim, a Bayer matrix, a wet smudge, a cross-stitch sew-in, rising ink smoke, slat blinds, an opaque-run pixel sort, and a hole that reveals a second layer. GSAP’s leftover work is structure the ten shipped tweens never touch: a split-flap hinge, baseline tracking, a roll onto a second face, a scribble that draws and undraws, letters moving onto a circle, a word-shaped marquee, a diagonal crack, a masked letter wipe, and a corner flap. Two follow behaviors are still free, an overshoot with no ghost copies and a lagged swing with in-plane rotation, plus a flat color that tracks distance. Scale, shake, a circle growing from the pointer, a torn RGB slice, letters falling to a floor, and strips flipping over are already in the lab under other names.

## A rim, a matrix, and a thread belong on canvas

The Canvas cards worth building first are the ones that already match the lab’s two knobs. Radius is the disc, the brush, the spawn field, or the band. Strength is the second number the sources actually publish: rim offset, refraction factor, Bayer level count, stamp alpha, stitch-cell size, puff rate, how close a slat’s `scaleY` gets to closed, or how wide a brightness window is. Arcaweb’s droplet is the glass algorithm in Canvas 2D. For each pixel, a normalized distance from the rounded edge feeds `f = 1 + k * (1 - norm)^2` in the published code block, and the sample is `center + (pixel - center) * f`. Negative `k` around `-0.3` is their convex example. The center, where the norm is 1, is unbent. A white rim is painted from that same norm. A droplet the size of the card collapses into Lens, and letter counters pinch shut, so the droplet stays smaller than the mark and the card keeps untouched artwork around it ([Arcaweb](https://www.arcaweb.ch/en/recreate-liquid-glass-effect-html-canvas)).

The chromatic card is the radial cousin of that droplet, not the full-frame channel slide. The sourced full-frame gist replaces one channel with the same channel `intensity` pixels to the right across the whole image, which on a card reads as Glitch without the slices ([lqt0223](https://gist.github.com/lqt0223/8a258b68ae1c032fa1fb1e26c4965e8d)). The missing fidget samples red, green, and blue at three strengths along the line from the lens center, with the offset scaled by distance so the middle stays clean. liquidGL describes the same concentration: aberration “disperses the red and blue channels either side of the refraction vector” and “vanishes at the flat centre” ([liquidGL](https://github.com/naughtyduk/liquidGL)). Two to six pixels of rim offset is the useful strength. Past that, a small logo turns to mud and serifs double. Glass and the chromatic rim can share a disc. They should still be two cards, because one bends the sample and the other splits channels, and strength means `k` in one and a pixel offset in the other.

Bayer is the dither that is not Halftone. A tiled matrix, indexed by `x mod n` and `y mod n`, turns intermediate tones into a stable dot pattern. Szabados’s multi-level form keeps brand color: with `c` grey levels, each channel is quantized after the matrix offset is added ([Bayer Dithering, 2022](https://spencerszabados.github.io/blog/2022/bayer-dithering/)). Strength is the level count, 2 for a harsh black-and-white and 4–8 to keep the mark. An 8×8 cell bigger than a small logo destroys it, so logos use 4×4. Floyd–Steinberg and Atkinson smear error into neighboring pixels and leak dots into letter counters, so the card should stay ordered Bayer ([javaspring.net](https://www.javaspring.net/blog/monochrome-dithering-in-javascript-bayer-atkinson-floyd-steinberg/)). The 2025–2026 “retro dither” lens is a shader. The Canvas port is the still matrix inside the radius, feathered back to the original. Awarded plasma-dither hovers are fluid simulations and are a different, rejected effect ([Awwwards, Outsource Consultants](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/interactive-dither-effect-outsource-consultants-inc)).

Smudge, stitch, smoke, blinds, and sort are the other Canvas builds that survive both subjects if a clean buffer and an alpha test are mandatory. The paint demos are destructive. The lab version keeps an untouched bitmap, stamps a feathered patch along the pointer at partial alpha, and on leave fades the live buffer home ([Simple Paint Demo](https://math.hws.edu/graphicsbook/demos/c2/SimplePaintDemo.html), [smudge brush](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/28197378/html5-canvas-javascript-smudge-brush-tool)). Cross-stitch is a photo-to-pattern pipeline turned into a hover: downsample, quantize opaque cells to a thread color, draw an X, and sew cells in across the radius. The pipeline sources are pattern tools, not hovers ([PhotoToPattern](https://dev.to/wei_zhu_aa04d872270a4135c/how-i-turned-photos-into-printable-cross-stitch-patterns-in-the-browser-2ma7)). Smoke spawns soft sprites on opaque pixels, with an upward force and alpha tied to life, and does not delete the source pixel ([Middlebury, fall 2024](https://www.cs.middlebury.edu/~candrews/classes/cs467-f24/tutorials/tutorial06-smoke)). Blinds cut the bitmap with the nine-argument `drawImage` and scale each band toward its center. The famous venetian versions rotate strips in 3D to a second image ([The New Code](https://thenewcode.com/776/3d-venetian-blind-image-transition-in-pure-css)). That rotation is 3D flip. The Canvas card only squashes, and `scaleY` should clamp near 0.15 so a word does not vanish. Pixel sort reorders luminance inside a short band and must end a run when alpha drops, or a letter’s stem gets painted across the counter ([Emergent Mind](https://www.emergentmind.com/visualizations/pixel-sorting)).

Sketch, Voronoi, metaballs, and the sheen are worth a card on one subject. The sketch is grayscale, an inverted blur, and color-dodge, `base / (1 - blend / 255)`, masked to the radius. Without internal edges a flat logo goes white ([JsonKit](https://jsokit.com/blog/posts/image-to-sketch-gaussian-blur-and-color-dodge-blending-with-canvas/)). Voronoi fills each cell with the average of the opaque pixels nearest a seed and darkens the border between the nearest and second-nearest seed ([Lumitree](https://lumitree.art/blog/voronoi-diagram)). Metaballs sum `radius² / distance²` and paint where the sum passes 1 ([slicker.me](https://slicker.me/javascript/metaballs/metaballs-tutorial.htm)). Apollo’s sheen is a translucent slab, rotated about 45°, traveling from above the picture to below it ([Codrops set 2](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set2.css)). Posterize, the flat quantization with the Bayer matrix removed, does not earn its own card. On a brand logo that is already a few flat colors, the only motion is a jagged anti-aliased edge, and Bayer already owns quantization.

## Headlines still hinge, track, and roll a second face

The headline cards are GSAP because the lab’s headline is already one element per letter. The split flap is the clearest miss. vawe.dev’s flap is a resolve that rolls through other characters, and that roll is the lab’s scramble. The hinge is not. Each glyph is four layers, a tile about 0.72em by 1.1em, perspective 3em, the top half rotating shut on `transform-origin: 50% 100%` and the bottom half swinging up, 34 ms each way, a shade reaching 0.6 opacity when the flap is edge-on, and a 0.016em split line. vawe’s own failure note is the lab’s 3D flip: rotating the whole glyph keeps it one piece, `rotateX` without perspective only squashes, and there is no shade ([vawe](https://vawe.dev/moves/flap-resolve)). On enter, flap the current character once. On leave, flap back. Do not cycle a charset.

Tracking is the width effect that does not need a variable font. CodeFronts eases `letter-spacing` from `0.02em` to `0.35em` in about 350 ms ([CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/css-letter-spacing-expand-hover-effect/)). On the lab’s per-letter elements, GSAP moves each letter’s `x` to `(index - center) * gap` and back to 0. No rotation. Proximity `wght` and `wdth` are real and well documented, and they are the wrong family for this lab. magnetType states the effect is invisible without a variable font, a `wght` change has no honest transform fallback, and `scaleY` is dock zoom ([magnetType](https://github.com/Liiift-Studio/MagnetType)). A `wdth` axis also reflows the line. The tracking card is the fallback, and it is the one to build.

The second-face roll is Hakim El Hattab’s rolling link, as shipped in Codrops’ 2013 component: the word rotates on its top edge to `rotateX(90deg)` and a second copy that rested at `rotateX(-90deg)` arrives in another color ([Creative Link Effects](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css)). The scribble is the May 2025 DrawSVG demo, a hand-drawn stroke from `"0%"` to `"100%"` in 0.5 s on enter and `"100% 100%"` on leave. DrawSVG is free with GSAP 3.13, and the core fallback is `stroke-dasharray` plus `stroke-dashoffset` on a path under the word, not along the glyph ([Codrops, 14 May 2025](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/05/14/from-splittext-to-morphsvg-5-creative-demos-using-free-gsap-plugins/)). The masked wipe uses the same article’s SplitText `mask: "lines"` idea, rebound from the published click to enter and leave, with a hand-built overflow wrapper so the page does not need the plugin. The diagonal crack is Urpi: two polygons, one `(0,0)-(width,0)-(width,height)` and the other `(0,0)-(0,height)-(width,height)`, nudged by a few pixels in opposite directions. The published offset is about 4 px and 1 px, so strength has to scale it or the card looks like a flicker ([Text Styles](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/index.html)).

Circular type and the marquee are adaptations, and they are still worth a headline card because nothing shipped moves whole letters onto a closed path or scrolls a repeated word through a fixed window. Codrops’ circular SVG text, 3 March 2021, is an intro, not a hover. The lab version tweens letters onto the circle and back to the baseline, which those demos do not do ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2021/03/03/circular-svg-text-animation/)). Awwwards files marquee-on-hover as its own element type and confirms enter and mouseout. The pages do not describe a clip inside the letterforms, so the window-the-width-of-the-word is the lab’s shaping of that pattern ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/scrolling-marquee-text-on-hover)). Spring sag is the physics card that stays off the floor. Edgeless Lab gives each letter gravity, an inverse-square pointer push, a spring home, and circle collision at restitution 0.7, and says the spring is what keeps the word a word ([Edgeless Lab](https://edgelesslab.com/blog/kinetic-type-physics/)). Pretext’s gravity letters and Matter.js drops are Drop. If the spring is weak, this card is Drop too.

The corner flap is the logo-side GSAP card. GSAP Vault’s sticker peel runs on GSAP core: a flap, a reflection matrix, `transform-origin: 0 0`, and a drop shadow ([GSAP Vault](https://gsapvault.com/effects/sticker-peel)). Canvas UI’s cylindrical peel is WebGL and a new renderer ([Peel](https://canvasui.dev/docs/components/peel)). curl.js can curl an image in Canvas 2D, and a strip-fold built from `drawImage` is an inference from the WebGL cylinder plus rectangle-only `drawImage`, not a line reading of curl.js ([curl.js](http://www.netzgesta.de/curl/)). Build the GSAP flap. It loses the cylinder, the lip highlight, and a true backside, and it still reads as a peel on a square mark.

## Follow and color stay new when scale and skew stay home

Three GSAP motions look dangerous because the lab already follows the pointer, and they stay new if the property list is guarded. Overshoot follow is not a single published demo. It is `gsap.quickTo` with an ease that passes the target. The official follower uses `power3`, which settles without overshoot. The May 2025 drag thread uses `elastic.out` for a return that springs past rest. Putting that ease on `x` and `y`, and not on scale, is the missing combination ([quickTo](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.quickTo()/), [forum, 26 May 2025](https://gsap.com/community/forums/topic/44625-need-the-characters-spring-back-to-their-original-position-after-dragging-using-draggable-and-inertia-plug-in-not-working-need-help/)). Leave sends every piece to 0. Echo is the card that would be rebuilt if the pieces also left copies behind.

Lagged swing is a published hover. Codrops, 1 July 2020, reveals a menu image and then, while the item is hovered, sets `x`, `y`, `rotation`, and `filter: brightness(...)` from lerped pointer values ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2020/07/01/creating-a-menu-image-animation-on-hover/)). The published subject is a photo beside menu text. The same four properties on each letter, or on a logo treated as one piece, are the card. Rotation here is in-plane. The moment it becomes `rotationX` and `rotationY` aimed at the pointer, it is 3D flip. The moment the horizontal component is `skewX` from velocity, it is smear.

Distance color is the part of the 14 May 2025 dot grid that is not the lab’s inertia card. On move, `t = max(0, 1 - dist / threshold)` and `gsap.utils.interpolate` writes a flat `color` or `backgroundColor`. Past the threshold the piece is the base color again, so leaving restores it ([Codrops, 14 May 2025](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/05/14/from-splittext-to-morphsvg-5-creative-demos-using-free-gsap-plugins/)). The lab’s radius is that threshold. The same article’s inertia push and click shockwave should be dropped. The x-ray hole is the Canvas companion: two layers, a radial mask or `destination-out` circle spring-following the pointer, and a second string or a second-color mark underneath. shadcn.io cites stiffness 250 and damping 30 ([Xray Text](https://www.shadcn.io/text/xray-text)). If the revealed art is the same pixels recolored, the card is Spotlight and should not be built.

## The famous leftovers are cards the lab already ships

The catalogs are large, and most of the famous names are a shipped card. hover.css Grow, Push, Pulse, and the April 2026 “lift” are GSAP elastic and Dock zoom. Buzz and wobble are GSAP wiggle. A 4° rotate and the roundup “tilt” are 3D tilt. A held `skew(-10deg)` is smear standing still. Radial Out, Rectangle Out, and the CodeFronts iris grow a mask from the center and are Spotlight. hover.css Glow is Glow. “Fall down,” staggered letter rise, and “split text” are Drop and Cascade. Nukun’s `rotateY(180deg)`, Yaku’s full turn per letter, and the 3D venetian blind are 3D flip sliced or staggered ([hover.css](https://ianlunn.github.io/Hover/), [The New Code](https://thenewcode.com/776/3d-venetian-blind-image-transition-in-pure-css)). Terminal shuffle, Quai’s `shuffleChars`, and Brilliant Digital’s glitch-plus-digit-scramble 404 are scramble and Glitch ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/glitch-chromatic-aberration-404-with-digit-scramble-brilliant-digital-2)). Asiri’s jolting grid is wiggle on a background. Repetition layers are elastic scale stacked. nxworld blur is Focus. Magnetic buttons and the October 2025 shader ripples are Magnetic and Liquid ripple. Evervault’s radial gradient mask, 17 May 2023, is Spotlight ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/05/17/recreating-the-gradient-mask-hover-effect-from-evervault/)). Alena Orlova’s skewed grid, 13 September 2023, is smear ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/09/13/grid-item-hover-effect/)). Robin Payot’s June 2023 WebGL bulge is Bulge ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/06/28/creating-a-bulge-distortion-effect-with-webgl/)).

A second set is new in the wild and still the wrong build. The 18 January 2026 canvas “liquid distortion” tutorial shifts `drawImage` source rectangles away from the pointer and eases them back. It is Bulge plus Jelly ([freefrontend](https://freefrontend.com/code/interactive-canvas-liquid-image-distortion-2026-01-18/)). Full-frame chromatic shift is the gist above. Repelling image particles that spring home are Scatter. Pointillism is Halftone. Quai Network’s alternating clip slices, 21 June 2023, are a real hover, and they should not be a second strip card next to slat blinds. The published rest state also hides the image, and the article’s character shuffle is scramble ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/06/21/coding-the-sliced-image-hover-effect-from-quai-network/)). The page-scale gooey cursor of 7 June 2023 is an SVG filter on the whole page, and the 2020 gooey menu morph sits next to Jelly. Metaball goop is the card-local version of that fuse. Image trails, the 2019 Three.js plane and the later DOM spawn-by-distance versions, read as Echo once they are shrunk to one card: copies of the mark fading along the pointer. Physics2D letters that leave the viewport restate Shatter and Explode. Elastic drag-home is a press, and the lab’s gesture is leave. MorphSVG and MotionPath need an SVG path the raster tiles and HTML letters do not have. Glyph stroke-draw, inner-stroke fill, and a stitch that walks a logo outline fail for the same reason: a PNG has no path, `strokeText` dashes in screen space, and tracing the bitmap invents a different mark ([Codrops, 31 January 2023](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/01/31/bringing-letters-to-life-coding-a-kinetic-svg-typography-animation/)).

The award sites do not add a renderer. Dorian Lods’ 2025 refraction hover, liquidGL, and Apple’s June 2025 Liquid Glass are GPU or live-page lenses. The card-local sample remap is the glass card above. PANAMÆRA’s smoke cursor and the Codapress dither plasma are shaders. The particle puff and the still Bayer matrix are the ports. Canvas UI’s peel, the June 2026 Three.js paint reveal, and LO2S’s 2025 logo shaders stay out. Cloth was considered and set aside: a Verlet sheet is a new simulation, and on a small logo it looks like Jelly ([Cylian](https://cylian.org/canvas/cloth/)). Slit-scan was set aside because a still logo reproduces itself until the pointer’s path becomes the time axis, and sparse letters write empty strips ([thewhodidthis](https://thewhodidthis.com/slit-scan-thing/)). Kinetic rows that follow the pointer, Codrops 29 May 2024, were set aside because one pass read them as Wave, the article’s tween was not in the fetched text, and lagged swing already owns a delayed `x`. FLIP flex was set aside because the published pen grows a cell inside a grid of neighbors, and a single lab card has no neighboring cells to reflow ([GreenSock pen](https://codepen.io/GreenSock/pen/jEbpKYG)). Zoom-in-a-crop, a 5 px float, grayscale, and a growing rectangular border are real, missing, and too plain to spend a card on.

## Conclusion

The lab has finished the transform vocabulary. Scale, skew, spin, throw, orbit, and flip are shipped, so a new GSAP card earns its place only when the motion is a hinge, a clip, a stroke under the word, a second face, or a flat fill by distance. The Canvas work that is still open is local resampling: touch the rectangle around the pointer, ignore pixels below an alpha cutoff, and tie the smallest feature (Bayer cell, stitch, Voronoi seed, metaball radius, channel offset) to strength so it stays smaller than the logo and the letter stroke. Glass and the chromatic rim are the 2025–2026 looks that survive that test without WebGL. Everything else pitched as liquid, glitch, or slices should be checked against Bulge, Jelly, Glitch, and the slat card before it is allowed a tile.
