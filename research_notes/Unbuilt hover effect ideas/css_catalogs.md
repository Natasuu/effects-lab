# CSS and Codrops hover catalogs

Scope: named effects from hover.css (Ian Lunn, v2), Codrops / Tympanus image and text hovers, CSS-Tricks link articles, the nxworld image-hover set, and 2024–2026 roundups. Compared with the lab as of 10 October 2026. The lab already has, on one card, enter-to-play and leave-to-restore: Canvas Pixel burst, Jelly, Magnetic, Liquid ripple, Shatter, Glitch, Halftone, Scatter, Sand, Swirl, Lens, Wave, Pixelate, Highlighter, ASCII, 3D tilt, Dissolve, Black hole, Echo, Focus, Filings, Bulge, Pinch, Glow, Pluck, Confetti, Melt, Spotlight, Drop, Dock zoom, VHS, Duotone; GSAP elastic scale, explode, scramble, 3D flip, wiggle, inertia throw, swap places, orbit, cascade stagger, velocity smear. Shared knobs are radius and strength. No new libraries. A survivor has to play on a logo cut into pieces or a headline split into letters, then return the artwork when the pointer leaves.

Primary files read for technique: hover.css mixins linked below; Codrops [set1.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set1.css) and [set2.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set2.css) as cited in the prior pass and not re-opened this pass except where a new claim is made; [Creative Link Effects `component.css`](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css); the full [Text Styles `linkstyles.css`](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css); Urpi’s polygons in [index.html](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/index.html); the Quai slice tutorial’s enter and leave timelines.

## Which named effects from hover.css, Codrops, and major 2024–2026 hover roundups are still missing?

### Takeaway

The catalogs are large, and most names are a scale, a shake, a caption, or a circle that grows. What the lab still does not have is a short list of wipes, frames, type rules, a second-face roll, a diagonal letter crack, a scan bar, letter halves, a traveling sheen, tracking, a self-drawing stroke, a squiggle, and ordered image strips. 2024–2026 roundups mostly rename that set. The new Codrops posts in 2025–2026 are page layouts or WebGL.

### Cited Findings

- hover.css v2 groups effects as 2D transitions, background transitions, border transitions, icons, shadow and glow, speech bubbles, and curls. The demo’s 2D names include Grow, Shrink, Pulse, Pulse Grow, Pulse Shrink, Push, Pop, Bounce In, Bounce Out, Rotate, Grow Rotate, Float, Sink, Bob, Hang, Skew, Wobble variants, Buzz, and Buzz Out, plus icon versions of the same verbs. — [demo](https://ianlunn.github.io/Hover/); [README](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/README.md)
- Mixins opened, and what they do:
  - Sweep To Right: a `::before` color layer `scaleX(0)` to `scaleX(1)` with origin at the left edge. — [`_sweep-to-right.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_sweep-to-right.scss)
  - Shutter Out Horizontal: the same color layer, `scaleX` from the center. One band, not venetian blinds. — [`_shutter-out-horizontal.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_shutter-out-horizontal.scss)
  - Radial Out: a full-size `::before` with `border-radius: 100%` scales from `0` to `2`. Rectangle Out is the square version of that center grow. — [`_radial-out.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_radial-out.scss); [`_rectangle-out.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_rectangle-out.scss)
  - Ripple Out: a solid rectangular border moves outward and fades. No border-radius in the mixin. — [`_ripple-out.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/border-transitions/_ripple-out.scss)
  - Outline Out grows the border’s offsets. Trim fades an inset border in. Hollow drops the background and keeps an inset shadow. Underline From Center grows a 4px bar from the middle. — [border-transitions](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/tree/master/scss/effects/border-transitions)
  - Curl Top Left grows a corner box painted with a 135deg gradient and a 1px shadow. It is not a bent page. — [`_curl-top-left.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/curls/_curl-top-left.scss)
  - Float Shadow: `translateY(-5px)` plus a radial-gradient ellipse. Glow is `box-shadow: 0 0 8px`. — [`_float-shadow.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/shadow-and-glow-transitions/_float-shadow.scss); [`_glow.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/shadow-and-glow-transitions/_glow.scss)
  - Grow is `scale(1.1)`. Rotate is `rotate(4deg)`. Skew is `skew(-10deg)`. Push scales to `0.8` and back. Buzz is an infinite `translateX(3px) rotate(2deg)` at 0.15s. Wobble Horizontal is one decaying `translateX`. Bob and Hang are a tiny infinite `translateY`. Forward is `translateX(8px)`. — [2d-transitions](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/tree/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions); [`_forward.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_forward.scss)
- Codrops “Ideas for Subtle Hover Effects” (19 June 2014, set 2 on 28 October 2014) names Lily, Sadie, Honey, Layla, Zoe, Oscar, Marley, Ruby, Roxy, Bubba, Romeo, Dexter, Sarah, Chico, Milo, then Julia, Goliath, Hera, Winston, Selena, Terry, Phoebe, Apollo, Kira, Steve, Moses, Jazz, Ming, Lexi, Duke. Almost every one fades a caption. — [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2014/06/19/ideas-for-subtle-hover-effects/); [set 2 markup](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/index2.html)
- Direction-aware hover (Mary Lou, 9 April 2012) slides an overlay in from the side the pointer entered and out toward the side it left. Direction is `atan2` quantized to four sides. CSS-Tricks later collected the same pattern. — [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2012/04/09/direction-aware-hover-effect-with-css3-and-jquery/); [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/direction-aware-hover-effects/)
- Creative Link Effects (6 August 2013) names 21 link treatments: brackets; 3D rolling links credited to Hakim; several underlines and borders; a duplicate word sliding in; a text fill whose comment points at a Lea Verou dabblet; circles; a cross; fall down; two “3D side” rotations. The shipped rule for the text-fill effect is a second word whose `max-width` grows from 0 to 100%, not a moving gradient. — [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2013/08/06/creative-link-effects/); [component.css](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css)
- Text Styles (13 May 2015) names Kukuri, Takiri, Surinami, Nukun, Kumya, Manko, Yaku, Mallki, Ilin, Asiri, and Urpi. The stylesheet, read in full this pass:
  - Kukuri: a 16px bar translates from `-100%` to `100%` in 0.4s (`cubic-bezier(0.7,0,0.3,1)`). A second copy of the word, `attr(data-letters)`, grows `width` from 0 to 100% after a 0.3s delay.
  - Kumya: a full-height block translates from `101%` to 0 while a second copy reveals by width. Same family as Kukuri.
  - Takiri: a 36px-tall, 120% wide bar, rotated 45deg, `scaleX` from 0 to 1. A small caption fades in. This is an in-place diagonal band, not a traveling sheen.
  - Surinami: a 3px rule at the top grows from the right (`transform-origin: 100% 50%`) and a 3px rule at the bottom grows from the left (`transform-origin: 0 50%`). The original word goes `color: transparent`. Two copies, `data-letters-l` from `translateX(-5px)` and `data-letters-r` from `translateX(5px)`, slide to 0 and fade in.
  - Mallki: a 6px bar translates from `-100%` to `100%`. Two overflow-hidden spans, each 50% tall, hold copies of the word. The top copy starts at `translateY(100%)` and the bottom copy at `translateY(-100%)`; both go to 0 after a 0.3s delay.
  - Ilin: the word is two spans. Each has a full-size color cover. At rest the first cover is at `translateY(-150%)` and the second at `translateY(150%)`. On hover they swap those offsets, so the covers travel through the halves in opposite directions, and the two halves recolor (`#fff` and `#1e1a1b`). Duration 0.5s.
  - Urpi: two copies, clipped and nudged. The polygons are not horizontal halves. `#cp_up` is `points="0,0 310,0 310,160"` and `#cp_down` is `points="0,0 0,160 310,160"`, a diagonal from top-left to bottom-right. On hover the up copy goes `translate3d(4px,1px,0)` and the down copy `translate3d(-4px,-1px,0)` while the unclipped word goes transparent.
  - Manko: two horizontal double-borders (`border-width: 4px 0`) `scaleX` from 0 to 1, one inset from the sides. Small captions fade above and below. A frame, not a new letter motion.
  - Nukun: `rotate3d(0,1,0,180deg)` on the word, plus two short rules. Yaku: `rotateY` a full turn, staggered per letter, while a block wipes in.
  - Asiri: the word’s SVG fill goes transparent and a `stroke` of 1.5 stays. On `mouseenter`, a canvas starts a 23×10 grid of quads (cell 18px) whose corners random-walk inside ±10px and spring back; `mouseleave` cancels the loop. The article credits a “jolt” to Tiffany Rayside.
  — [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2015/05/13/inspiration-for-text-styles-and-hover-effects/); [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css); [index.html polygons and Asiri loop](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/index.html)
- Gooey, as used on the web, is an SVG filter: blur, then `feColorMatrix` boosting the alpha row (Codrops uses values ending `19 -9`; CSS-Tricks shows `18 -7` and `19 -9`), then the sharp graphic on top. Codrops shipped a morphing text hover on 28 April 2020 and a full-cursor version on 7 June 2023. — [Creative Gooey Effects, 10 March 2015](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2015/03/10/creative-gooey-effects/); [CSS-Tricks, Lucas Bebber](https://css-tricks.com/gooey-effect/); [text hover](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2020/04/28/morphing-gooey-text-hover-effect/); [cursor](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/06/07/gooey-cursor-effect/)
- Self-drawing lines are `stroke-dasharray` and `stroke-dashoffset` on a path. That is a path effect, not a filter. — [How SVG Line Animation Works](https://css-tricks.com/svg-line-animation-works/)
- nxworld’s image-hover set, as republished, includes Zoom In (`scale(1.3)` inside `overflow: hidden`), Zoom Out, Slide, Rotate plus zoom, Blur, Gray Scale, Sepia, Opacity, Flashing, and Circle. — [iamrohit.in](https://www.iamrohit.in/awesome-css-image-hover-effects/); [CodePen `nxworld/pen/ZYNOBZ`](https://codepen.io/nxworld/pen/ZYNOBZ)
- CSS-Tricks: an underline that thickens into a block (`box-shadow` inset from about `-0.07em` to `-0.85em` on the Superfriendly example); a squiggle underline from The Outline, drawn as an SVG path (the author of that squiggle, in the comments, lists the requirements as multiline, any color, and animate on hover); outlined text (fill removed, stroke left); raised text. Adam Argyle’s passing highlight scales in from the left and, because hover flips `transform-origin` to the right, leaves through the opposite side. — [block underlines](https://css-tricks.com/link-underlines-that-animate-into-block-backgrounds/); [squiggle, outline, raised](https://css-tricks.com/having-fun-with-link-hover-effects/); [passing highlight, 2021 writeup of Argyle](https://css-tricks.com/adam-argyles-sick-mouse-out-css-hover-effect/); [six link ideas](https://css-tricks.com/css-link-hover-effects/)
- Quai Network slices (Codrops, 21 June 2023, GSAP): the image is N clip-path strips (demos use 5 and 15, vertical or horizontal). On enter, odd strips come from `yPercent`/`xPercent` random between -75 and -25, even strips from 25 to 75, and both go to 0; the wrapper and the image counter-translate by 100%. On leave, the image goes back to 100% and opacity 0, and the strips go back out (the published odd-strip leave range is `random(-75, 25)`, the even range `random(25, 75)`). The same tutorial also shuffles characters. Idle state in the CSS hides the image (`.js .card__img { opacity: 0 }`). — [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/06/21/coding-the-sliced-image-hover-effect-from-quai-network/)

### Inferences

- “Missing” is not “worth a card.” After dropping scale, jitter, captions, and circle masks, the uncovered canon is edge wipes, a center shutter, frames and rules, a second-face roll, a diagonal crack, a scan-bar reveal, halves closing, a sheen, tracking, a drawn stroke, a squiggle, and ordered strips.
- 2024–2026 roundups are not a new canon. Shine, border draw, fill wipe, zoom, and letter-spacing are renames. The new Codrops posts add layouts and shaders.
- CodeFronts’ split curtain is the same opening as Shutter (two panels leaving the middle). Its iris is Radial Out. Only the multi-strip Quai motion is a different picture.

### Gaps

- Bounce In, Bounce Out, Float, Sink, and the icon-folder files were not opened one by one. Forward was opened and is an 8px slide. The icon names on the demo match the 2D verbs.
- nxworld “Slide” and “Circle” rules were not retrieved, only their names.
- The Lea Verou dabblet cited by Creative Link Effect 11 was not fetched. The shipped rule is `max-width`. Do not describe it as a gradient.
- Studio Limb’s demo source was not opened. “Ken Burns” and “spinning conic border” on the CodeFronts index were not opened as keyframes. The curtain page on that site was opened.
- Codrops grid-item reveal (March 2024) still does not describe its hover geometry in the fetched article text.
- Repetition sketch 004 (“Filter”) was not opened. The article text only establishes stacked scales for the set.
- Hakim’s rolling-links page (`http://hakim.se/thoughts/rolling-links`), cited in the CSS comment, was not fetched. The shipped CSS was.

## Which of those survive translation onto a logo or onto individual letters, rather than only working as a button background?

### Takeaway

Twenty named effects still work on one card, on a logo or on letters, in Canvas 2D or GSAP, and are not the same motion as a card already in the lab. Caption choreography, speech bubbles, icon slides, and full-page cursors do not. A cropped zoom, a 5px lift, and a grayscale filter are real and missing, and too plain to spend a card on.

### Cited Findings

Each survivor: what it looks like, where it works, original technique, and why it is not a duplicate. Implementation is in the next section.

1. **Sweep to right.** A second color covers the artwork from the left edge until it is full, and retreats on leave. Letters or logo pieces. CSS transform. Not Duotone or Spotlight: the mask is a slab from one edge, not a circle around the pointer. — [sweep mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_sweep-to-right.scss). The same motion is “fill wipe” and “button fill slide.” — [Studio Limb](https://www.studiolimb.com/guides/css-hover-effects-examples.html); [CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/)
2. **Shutter out horizontal.** The same color layer opens from the vertical center to both sides. Letters or logo. CSS. Not Sweep (origin is the center) and not Wave (no sine). The CodeFronts split curtain, two halves at `translateX(±100%)`, is this opening done with two panels. Build one. — [shutter mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_shutter-out-horizontal.scss); [curtain page](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/css-curtain-reveal-image-hover-effect/)
3. **Curl top left.** A small square grows in a corner, painted with a diagonal gradient so it reads as a peeled corner. Logo card, or the word’s box. CSS width and height. Not Shatter and not 3D tilt: a corner patch grows, nothing turns in perspective. — [curl mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/curls/_curl-top-left.scss)
4. **Ripple out.** A rectangular border steps outward and fades while the pointer is inside. It does not bend the picture. Logo frame or the word box. CSS. Not Liquid ripple: no height field and no pixel displacement. Creative Link Effect 12 is the round cousin, circles scaling up from the word. — [ripple mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/border-transitions/_ripple-out.scss); [component.css](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css)
5. **Underline from center, with two famous variants.** A 4px rule grows from the middle of the baseline. Thickening it to the em box is the CSS-Tricks block highlight. Flipping the origin on leave, so the rule exits the opposite side, is Adam Argyle’s passing highlight. Letters. CSS. Not Highlighter: one rule grows and holds, or passes through once. It is not a brush that follows the pointer. Strikethrough is the same rule through the x-height. — [underline mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/border-transitions/_underline-from-center.scss); [block background](https://css-tricks.com/link-underlines-that-animate-into-block-backgrounds/); [passing highlight](https://css-tricks.com/adam-argyles-sick-mouse-out-css-hover-effect/)
6. **Direction-aware slab.** On enter, a second treatment slides in from the side the pointer crossed; on leave it slides out the exit side. Logo. CSS plus direction math. Not Spotlight: the mask is an edge slab whose direction comes from entry and exit. — [Codrops, 2012](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2012/04/09/direction-aware-hover-effect-with-css3-and-jquery/); [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/direction-aware-hover-effects/)
7. **Brackets.** `[` and `]` fade and slide in against the sides of the word. Headline. CSS. Not a button frame: the marks are glyphs on the headline. Manko’s double horizontal rules are the same idea turned sideways; build brackets. — [Creative Link Effects](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2013/08/06/creative-link-effects/); [Manko in linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
8. **Rolling links.** The word rolls back on its top edge (`rotateX(90deg)`, origin at the top) and a second copy that was waiting at `rotateX(-90deg)` comes up in another color. Letters, as one word or per letter. CSS 3D, credited in the source to Hakim El Hattab. Not the lab’s 3D flip: flip tracks the pointer; this is one enter/leave roll onto a back face. — [component.css](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css); [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2013/08/06/creative-link-effects/)
9. **Kukuri.** A 16px bar sweeps across the word and off the other side, and a darker copy of the letters is revealed by width after a delay. Headline. CSS. Not Highlighter and not Sweep alone: the bar leaves, and the second color is a clip of the glyphs. Kumya is the same family with a full-height block. — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
10. **Mallki.** The hover-colored word is split into a top half and a bottom half. The halves slide together and a 6px bar crosses. Letters. CSS. Not Cascade (nothing staggers in from outside the card) and not Shatter (the halves meet and hold). — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
11. **Urpi.** The word is cut on the diagonal from top-left to bottom-right (the two polygons above). The two pieces nudge apart by 4px and 1px in opposite directions and recolor, then close on leave. Letters. CSS plus SVG clip. Not Glitch: two pieces, one straight cut, no cyan/magenta fringe and no random bands. Not Mallki: the cut is diagonal and the pieces separate instead of assembling. — [index.html](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/index.html); [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
12. **Surinami.** The top rule grows from the right, the bottom rule from the left, the original word disappears, and two copies slide in from 5px left and 5px right and stay. Letters. CSS. Not Echo: exactly two copies meet and hold. They do not trail the pointer. — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
13. **Ilin.** Two halves of the word. A color block travels down through the first half and up through the second, and each half keeps a new color after the block has left. Letters. CSS. Not Sweep: the covers move in opposite directions and do not stay on the word. Not Kukuri: there is no horizontal scan bar and no clipped second string. — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
14. **Hera.** A square outline, rotated -45deg, scales from 0 to 1 at the center of the picture. Logo. CSS. Not Spotlight: the aperture is a diamond frame, not a circle of recolored artwork following the pointer. — [set2.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set2.css)
15. **Apollo sheen.** A translucent white slab, rotated about 45deg, travels from above the image to below it. Logo. CSS. Not Spotlight, Glow, or Highlighter: one angled band crosses once. Jazz, and Takiri on a headline, open that 45deg band in place (`scaleX` from 0 to 1) instead of traveling. 2026 roundups call the traveling version shine or shimmer. Build the traveling one. — [set2.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set2.css); [Takiri](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css); [Studio Limb](https://www.studiolimb.com/guides/css-hover-effects-examples.html)
16. **Letter-spacing expand.** Letters move apart on the baseline and close on leave. Headline only. CSS `letter-spacing`. Not Explode: no rotation, no random directions, letters stay on the line. — [CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/)
17. **Border draw.** A rectangular stroke draws itself around the logo and undraws on leave. Logo card. SVG dash offset. Not Outline Out (that border jumps outward and stays) and not Glow. — [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/svg-line-animation-works/); [Studio Limb, April 2026](https://www.studiolimb.com/guides/css-hover-effects-examples.html)
18. **Squiggle.** A wavy line draws itself under the word and undraws on leave. The Outline’s version is an SVG path used as the link underline. Headline. SVG. Not Wave: the letters do not move. Not the straight underline: the path is a sine and it draws along its length. — [CSS-Tricks, 21 September 2018](https://css-tricks.com/having-fun-with-link-hover-effects/)
19. **Quai slices.** The picture is cut into 5 or 15 rigid strips. The strips slide in from alternating sides and lock; on leave they slide back out. Logo. CSS clip-path plus GSAP. Not Shatter: no rotation, the strips stay in their slots. Not Wave: one translate per strip, not a sine field. Not Glitch: no color fringe. Not Scatter: they only move on the axis across the cut. The tutorial also shuffles letters; that part is the lab’s scramble and should not be built. — [Codrops, 21 June 2023](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/06/21/coding-the-sliced-image-hover-effect-from-quai-network/)
20. **Gooey text.** Letters or soft logo pieces blur, the alpha is crushed so overlaps fuse, and on leave they separate. Headline or a soft logo. SVG filter originally. Not Jelly (no wobbling surface) and not Melt (no downward drip). The June 2023 gooey cursor is page-scale and should not be built. — [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/gooey-effect/); [text hover, 28 April 2020](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2020/04/28/morphing-gooey-text-hover-effect/)

Named, real, and too plain for their own card:

- **Zoom pan.** nxworld zoom-in is `scale(1.3)` with `overflow: hidden`. It survives on a logo. It is not Lens, Dock zoom, or elastic scale. It is still one scale inside a crop. Ken Burns, as a roundup name, is this plus a slow pan. Build it only if a non-springing crop is wanted. — [iamrohit.in](https://www.iamrohit.in/awesome-css-image-hover-effects/); [CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/)
- **Float shadow.** A 5px lift and an ellipse. Not Drop and not Dock zoom, and too small beside them. — [mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/shadow-and-glow-transitions/_float-shadow.scss)
- **Grayscale / sepia.** One CSS filter on the whole picture. Not Duotone and not Focus. One of them is enough if a filter card is wanted. — [iamrohit.in](https://www.iamrohit.in/awesome-css-image-hover-effects/)
- **Outlined word.** CSS-Tricks strips the fill and leaves a stroke. Asiri does the same with SVG `fill: transparent` and `stroke-width: 1.5`. A one-step fade, next to border draw. — [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/having-fun-with-link-hover-effects/); [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)

What does not survive as artwork motion:

- Speech bubbles add a tail. The artwork does not change. — [demo class list](https://ianlunn.github.io/Hover/)
- Hollow and Trim change a button’s fill. On a raster logo that is not a visible motion. — [hollow](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/border-transitions/_hollow.scss); [trim](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/border-transitions/_trim.scss)
- Sadie, Honey, Oscar, Marley, Ruby, Roxy, Bubba, Layla, Dexter, Sarah, Chico, Milo, Julia, Winston, Selena, Terry, Phoebe, Kira, Moses, Ming, Lexi: the picture barely moves and the event is the caption. Romeo’s X and Creative Link Effect 18 are two hairlines rotating to 45deg, decoration on top of the picture. Goliath, Steve, and Duke are caption-grid moves that collapse into a short pan or a scale. — [set1.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set1.css); [set2.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set2.css)
- Full-page or multi-item hovers: pointer-following rows (29 May 2024), a product grid that becomes a preview (27 May 2025), the gooey cursor (7 June 2023), a nav mask that resizes across links (5 March 2026). — [rows](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2024/05/29/hover-motion-intro-animation/); [product grid](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/05/27/animated-product-grid-preview-with-gsap-clip-path/); [cursor](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/06/07/gooey-cursor-effect/); [nav mask](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/03/05/inside-corentin-bernadous-portfolio-swiss-inspired-layouts-webgl-geometry-and-thoughtful-motion/)

### Inferences

- Type catalogs survive more often than the Codrops image names. From Lily through Duke, keep Hera and Apollo. The rest is caption timing.
- Radius fits almost none of these. They are enter/leave states. Strength maps onto distance, thickness, scale, or how far a strip travels. The extra knobs are edge, axis, corner, thickness, and angle.
- Urpi’s published offset is a hairline on a 10em word. Strength has to multiply those 4px or the card looks like a flicker.

### Gaps

- Hera’s and Apollo’s exact pixel values are from the set2 stylesheet as read in the earlier pass (200px diamond, white at about 0.5, travel from above to below). They were not re-opened this pass.
- “Spinning conic border” is a CodeFronts index name. Its keyframes were not opened, so it is not in the twenty.

## What is the minimal Canvas 2D or GSAP approach for each survivor?

### Takeaway

Every survivor is a GSAP transform on letters or on one logo group, or a Canvas 2D clip, stroke, or composite. None need a new library. Gooey is the only one whose famous version is an SVG filter; Canvas 2D can approximate it with blur plus an alpha threshold. A pen that traces the letter outlines cannot be done with the current pieces. The June 2026 paint-on-hover is a shader and should not be ported.

### Cited Findings

Shared knobs: radius is unused unless noted. Strength is the amplitude. “Extra” is a knob the shared pair does not cover.

1. **Sweep.** GSAP: a color layer `scaleX` from 0 to 1, `transformOrigin` left, reversed on leave. Canvas: grow a clip rect and redraw the logo in the hover color inside it. Strength = how far the wipe travels, or the mix. Extra: edge (hover.css has four sweeps). — [sweep mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_sweep-to-right.scss)
2. **Shutter.** Same layer with `transformOrigin: "50% 50%"` and `scaleX`, or `scaleY` for the vertical shutter. The curtain variant is two rects translating to ±100% of their width. Strength = how far it opens. Extra: axis. — [shutter mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_shutter-out-horizontal.scss)
3. **Curl.** Canvas: tween a corner rectangle from 0 to a small square, fill it with a diagonal gradient (page color, a dark crease, the underside) and a 1px shadow, tween it back on leave. Strength = side length. Extra: which corner. Do not simulate a curled mesh. — [curl mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/curls/_curl-top-left.scss)
4. **Ripple out.** Canvas: while the pointer is inside, stroke a rounded rect, or a circle for effect 12, whose inset grows and whose alpha hits 0. Spawn the next ring on a timer. Strength = how far the ring travels. Extra: rect versus circle. No pixel warp. — [ripple mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/border-transitions/_ripple-out.scss)
5. **Underline.** GSAP one rect under the baseline, `scaleX` 0 to 1 from the center. A second tween can grow its height from 4px to the em box. For the passing variant, enter from the left and, on leave, exit to the right instead of reversing. Strength = final height. Extra: origin, end height, and whether leave reverses or continues. — [underline mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/border-transitions/_underline-from-center.scss); [Argyle](https://css-tricks.com/adam-argyles-sick-mouse-out-css-hover-effect/)
6. **Direction-aware.** On enter, take the quadrant the way Codrops does (`atan2`, four sides). GSAP the overlay from just outside that edge to `x: 0, y: 0`. On leave, tween it out the exit edge. The overlay is a second draw of the logo, clipped to the card. Extra: overlay color. — [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/direction-aware-hover-effects/)
7. **Brackets.** GSAP two glyphs, `x` from about ±20px to 0 and opacity 0 to 1, reversed on leave. Strength = travel. — [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2013/08/06/creative-link-effects/)
8. **Rolling links.** GSAP on the word or each letter: `transformOrigin: "50% 0%"`, perspective, `rotateX` to 90. A second copy sits as the bottom face (`rotateX(-90)` at rest) in the other color. On leave, `rotateX` back to 0. Strength = degrees if a partial roll is wanted; the original is 90. Extra: face color. Do not aim the rotation at the pointer. — [component.css](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css)
9. **Kukuri.** GSAP a 16px bar from `xPercent: -100` to `100` in about 0.4s. Clip a second color of the letters and tween that clip’s width from 0 to 100% after about 0.3s. Reverse both on leave. Strength = bar thickness. — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
10. **Mallki.** For each letter, two clipped halves in the hover color. GSAP the top half’s `y` from its own height to 0 and the bottom half’s `y` from minus its height to 0. A 6px bar can cross at the same time. Strength = how far the halves travel. — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
11. **Urpi.** Canvas: draw the word twice. Clip one copy to the triangle `(0,0)-(width,0)-(width,height)` and the other to `(0,0)-(0,height)-(width,height)`. GSAP the two copies by opposite offsets. The source offset is `(4, 1)` and `(-4, -1)`; strength should scale that or it will not read. On leave, offsets back to 0 and the unsplit word returns. Extra: which diagonal. Keep it to two pieces. — [index.html](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/index.html)
12. **Surinami.** GSAP the top rule `scaleX` from 0 to 1 with origin on the right, and the bottom rule with origin on the left. Fade the original letters out. Two copies move from `x: -5` and `x: 5` to 0. Strength = the starting offset. — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
13. **Ilin.** Split the headline into two groups. GSAP a cover the size of the first group from `yPercent: -150` to `150`, and the second cover from `150` to `-150`, so each block passes through and leaves. Tween the two fills to their hover colors during the pass, and reverse the whole timeline on leave. Strength = travel, which the source sets at 150% of the span. — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
14. **Hera.** Canvas: stroke a square rotated -45deg, scaling from 0 to 1 at the card center. Optionally clip the hover color of the logo to that diamond. Strength = final size. Extra: rotation. — [set2.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set2.css)
15. **Apollo sheen.** Canvas: a wide translucent gradient rect, rotated about 45deg, moved from above the card to below, then back on leave. Clip it to the logo’s alpha so the band only tints the mark. Strength = opacity. Extra: angle. The in-place cousin (Jazz, Takiri) is the same rect with `scaleX` 0 to 1 and no travel. — [set2.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set2.css); [Takiri](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css)
16. **Tracking.** GSAP each letter’s `x` to `(index - center) * gap`. On leave, `x` back to 0. No rotation. Strength = gap. — [CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/)
17. **Border draw.** Canvas rectangle: `setLineDash([perimeter, perimeter])` and tween `lineDashOffset` from the perimeter to 0 on enter, and back on leave. Strength = stroke width. Extra: corner radius. This is the rectangle’s perimeter. It is not a trace of the logo’s contours. — [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/svg-line-animation-works/)
18. **Squiggle.** Canvas: build a sine polyline under the baseline, measure its length, and use the same dash-offset tween as border draw so the wave draws on enter and undraws on leave. Strength = amplitude. Extra: wavelength. The letters stay still. — [CSS-Tricks squiggle](https://css-tricks.com/having-fun-with-link-hover-effects/); [dash offset](https://css-tricks.com/svg-line-animation-works/)
19. **Quai slices.** Canvas: `drawImage` the logo into N horizontal or vertical strips. GSAP each strip’s cross-axis offset. Rest is aligned, because the lab’s logo has to be visible before the pointer arrives. On enter, odd strips move to a negative fraction and even strips to a positive fraction (the source draws those fractions from about 25% to 75% of the strip). On leave, every strip returns to 0. Do not fade the logo out, which is what the published idle state does, and do not shuffle characters. Strength = the offset. Extra: slice count and axis. Radius unused. — [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/06/21/coding-the-sliced-image-hover-effect-from-quai-network/)
20. **Gooey.** Canvas approximation, not a new library: draw the pieces, `ctx.filter = blur(radius)`, then threshold alpha. The `feColorMatrix` alpha row is what crushes the blur into blobs; CSS-Tricks gives that row as 18 and -7, or 19 and -9. GSAP the pieces toward their neighbors on enter so the blobs merge, and home on leave. Draw the sharp graphic on top if the letters should stay readable inside the blob. Extra knobs that matter more than the shared radius: blur radius and threshold. The original is an SVG filter. The canvas version will not match `feColorMatrix` exactly. — [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/gooey-effect/); [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2015/03/10/creative-gooey-effects/)

Blocked, even though they are famous:

- **Glyph stroke-draw.** `stroke-dashoffset` needs vector outlines. Canvas `strokeText` plus `setLineDash` dashes in screen space; it does not travel along the glyph. Doing it for real needs traced paths or a font parser, which is a new library. A rectangle is border draw. A sine under the word is the squiggle. A raster logo cannot grow a pen stroke without tracing. — [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/svg-line-animation-works/)
- **WebGL displacement, shader ripple, shader blur, and the June 2026 paint reveal.** The October 2025 tutorial drives ripples with shader uniforms. The 11 June 2026 portfolio paints a sketch texture into a colored texture with a noise threshold in a Three.js fragment shader, and GSAP only animates `uProgress`. A ragged wipe can be faked in Canvas, and it would be Sweep with a noisy edge, which the lab does not need as its own card. Liquid ripple, Bulge, Pinch, and Wave already cover the displacement looks that Canvas 2D can fake. Do not add a GL path. — [8 October 2025](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/10/08/how-to-animate-webgl-shaders-with-gsap-ripples-reveals-and-dynamic-blur-effects/); [11 June 2026](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/11/sketching-the-impossible-a-3d-portfolio-built-without-a-single-3d-model/)

### Inferences

- GSAP fits anything that is “move these letters”: brackets, roll, tracking, underline, Kukuri’s clip, Mallki’s halves, Ilin’s covers, Surinami’s twins, the direction-aware slab.
- Canvas 2D fits anything that is “paint over the bitmap”: sweep, shutter, curl, ripple ring, sheen, diamond, squiggle, strip offsets, border dash, gooey threshold.
- Radius earns a place on grayscale, which is not in the twenty, and nowhere else here. Using it on a wipe would turn Sweep into Spotlight.

### Gaps

- The terminal-type article (19 June 2024) describes a cursor variation separate from the shuffle. The cursor’s path was not in the fetched text. The shuffle matches GSAP scramble and should not be built. — [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2024/06/19/hover-animations-for-terminal-like-typography/)
- Double-image clip-path hovers (1 March 2023) mention a stepped invert and a glitch, without the polygon list. The glitch duplicates Glitch. The invert stays out until the polygons are read. — [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/03/01/double-image-hover-effects-with-clip-path-animations/)

## Which famous ones should be rejected because they duplicate an existing effect?

### Takeaway

Reject the hover.css 2D set, the circle and square center wipes, glow and buzz, venetian strip-flips, stacked scales, the WebGL renames, and the 2024–2026 glitch, tilt, magnetic, duotone, spotlight, and flip demos. They are the lab’s current cards under catalog names.

### Cited Findings

- **Scale family = GSAP elastic and Dock zoom.** Grow is `scale(1.1)`. Push scales to `0.8` and back. — [grow](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_grow.scss); [push](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_push.scss). Pulse, Pop, Shrink, Bounce In, and Bounce Out sit in that same 2D list; those five files were not opened. “Lift” and “scale and shadow” in the April 2026 roundup are this plus the 5px float. — [class list on the demo](https://ianlunn.github.io/Hover/); [Studio Limb](https://www.studiolimb.com/guides/css-hover-effects-examples.html)
- **Forward, Float, and Sink are an 8px slide, not a new motion.** Forward, opened, is `translateX(8px)`. Float and Sink were not opened; their names sit next to it in the 2D list. — [`_forward.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_forward.scss)
- **Buzz and wobble = GSAP wiggle.** Buzz is an infinite 0.15s translate-and-rotate. Wobble Horizontal is one decaying horizontal shake. — [buzz](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_buzz.scss); [wobble](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_wobble-horizontal.scss)
- **Rotate (4deg) and the roundup “tilt” = 3D tilt.** The hover.css rotate is a small in-plane turn, a flatter version of the lab’s tilt. — [rotate mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_rotate.scss); [Studio Limb](https://www.studiolimb.com/guides/css-hover-effects-examples.html); [CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/)
- **Skew = velocity smear, held still.** The mixin is `skew(-10deg)` for as long as the pointer is over the element. Smear already skews pieces from velocity. — [skew mixin](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_skew.scss)
- **Bob and Hang are not Wave.** They are a tiny infinite `translateY` of the whole element. — [bob](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_bob.scss); [hang](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/2d-transitions/_hang.scss)
- **Radial Out, Rectangle Out, and the CodeFronts iris = Spotlight.** All three grow a mask from the center until it covers the element. A square or a circle is the same motion. — [radial](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/background-transitions/_radial-out.scss); [iris](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/css-curtain-reveal-image-hover-effect/)
- **hover.css Glow = the lab’s Glow.** The opened mixin is `box-shadow: 0 0 8px`. The 2026 “glow outline” is the same halo. — [`_glow.scss`](https://github.com/IanLunn/Hover/blob/master/scss/effects/shadow-and-glow-transitions/_glow.scss); [Studio Limb](https://www.studiolimb.com/guides/css-hover-effects-examples.html)
- **Fall down, staggered letter rise, and “split text” = Drop and Cascade.** Creative Link Effect 16 is named “fall down.” Zoe brings a title up from `translate3d(0, 200%, 0)` with delays. Roundups still sell “split text” as a letter stagger. — [component.css](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css); [set1.css](https://github.com/codrops/HoverEffectIdeas/blob/master/css/set1.css); [CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/)
- **Nukun, Yaku, and the 3D venetian blind = 3D flip.** Nukun is `rotate3d(0,1,0,180deg)`. Yaku is `rotateY` through 360deg, staggered per letter, which is flip plus cascade. The New Code blinds are `rotateY(180deg)` on each vertical strip with a delay: the same flip, sliced. Creative Link Effects 19 and 20 are “3D side” rotations without the second face that makes rolling links different. — [linkstyles.css](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/css/linkstyles.css); [The New Code](https://thenewcode.com/776/3d-venetian-blind-image-transition-in-pure-css); [component.css](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css)
- **Shuffle, glitch type, and color-channel split = GSAP scramble and Glitch.** The June 2024 terminal shuffle and the Quai tutorial’s `shuffleChars` are character shuffling. The March 2023 clip-path set includes a glitch. The May 2026 portfolio’s chromatic aberration is the same fringe on a WebGL path. — [terminal](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2024/06/19/hover-animations-for-terminal-like-typography/); [Quai](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/06/21/coding-the-sliced-image-hover-effect-from-quai-network/); [6 May 2026](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/05/06/from-shader-uniforms-to-clip-path-wipes-how-gsap-drives-my-portfolio/)
- **Asiri’s jolt = wiggle, on a background grid.** The loop random-walks grid corners inside ±10px and springs them back, and it stops on leave. The letters themselves only lose their fill. That mesh is not a new letter motion. — [index.html](https://github.com/codrops/TextStylesHoverEffects/blob/master/index.html)
- **Repetition layers = elastic scale, stacked.** The February 2022 article animates repeated copies with `scale` / `scaleX` / `scaleY` and a stagger. That is the elastic card drawn more than once, not Echo, because the copies do not follow the pointer. — [article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2022/02/22/repetition-image-hover-effects/)
- **nxworld Blur = Focus. nxworld Flash is a blink. nxworld Rotate+Zoom = tilt plus a scale.** — [iamrohit.in](https://www.iamrohit.in/awesome-css-image-hover-effects/)
- **Magnetic buttons and shader ripples = Magnetic and Liquid ripple.** — [CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/); [WebGL article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/10/08/how-to-animate-webgl-shaders-with-gsap-ripples-reveals-and-dynamic-blur-effects/)
- **Duotone, spotlight, and stack-lift ghosts = Duotone, Spotlight, and Echo.** All three are named again on the CodeFronts roundup. — [CodeFronts](https://codefronts.com/motion/css-hover-effects/)
- **Effect 5, “same word slide in,”** is `translateY(-100%)` on a duplicate of the word. It is a vertical wipe of a second string, which Kukuri already covers. — [component.css](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css)
- **Effect 11’s shipped CSS** is a `max-width` reveal of a second word. That is Sweep on glyphs. — [component.css](https://github.com/codrops/CreativeLinkEffects/blob/master/css/component.css)
- **The June 2026 paint reveal** is Sweep with a noisy edge, and the implementation that was published is a Three.js shader. Do not add it as a card. — [11 June 2026](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/11/sketching-the-impossible-a-3d-portfolio-built-without-a-single-3d-model/)

### Inferences

- If a catalog name can be described as “it scales,” “it shakes,” “a circle grows from the center or the pointer,” “it glitches,” “the letters fall in,” or “strips flip over,” the lab already has it.
- The safe test: does the mark get covered from an edge, grow a rule, roll onto a second face, crack on a diagonal, or pull apart in ordered strips? Recoloring from the pointer is Duotone or Spotlight. Recoloring from an edge is Sweep, and that one is still free.

### Gaps

- Bounce In, Bounce Out, Float, Sink, and the icon files were not opened. If one of those mixins is something other than a small scale or translate, this pass missed it.
- nxworld Slide’s keyframes were not opened. If “Slide” is the image itself translating out to reveal a second image, it is direction-aware without the entry-side math, and it is already covered by survivor 6.
